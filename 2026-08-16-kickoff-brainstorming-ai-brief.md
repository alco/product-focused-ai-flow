# Kickoff brainstorming

Our job is to create a communications platform for hospitality businesses. They are trying to achieve two things:

1. Align their workforce and make them feel part of something bigger than just their day-to-day job
2. Replace WhatsApp for internal 1-to-1 and group communications

We're going to start by building the first version as an MVP. Let's brainstorm how much of each requirement must be a part of this first version and what each will look like.

## Re 1: align the workforce and make them feel part of something bigger

This is a hospitality business. Let's focus on the people that are working on-site, such as staff working at reception desks, cleaners, porters, possibly drivers, waiters, and managers that ensure everything is running smoothly. Offsite teams such as marketing, procurement, brand-identity, country managers and so on are out of scope here.

**What does aligning the workforce mean? What would make them feel part of something bigger?**

Beyond their day-to-day duties each worker cares about their collegues, the premises where they are working at and lastly the brand as a whole.

I'm thinking there needs to be a way to broadcast personal achievements and a sense of community by way of highting team effort with hard numbers and also celebrating events in people’s personal lives. Some ideas:

 - announcement channel for the location: selected company-wide news, local events (team building, charity, related, local news), staff members' birthdays and other life events
 - announcement channel for the entire company: most prominent company news, global events, brand recognition in the media, financial successes, etc.
 - a page with positive stats like numbers of customers served, customer satisfaction rating

This part of the brief is very open-ended and needs iteration with the people who are more familiar with the specific product area and/or know the customers' exact wishes or requirements. As such, we'll build out a basic version of this as part of the MVP and focus our main effort on the second point.

## Re 2: replace whatsapp for 1-1 and group comms

Assuming this is about the communications that happen between staff members and the management, NOT with the customers.

Apparently, our customers are trying to organize their communications using WhatsApp because they haven't found a better tool for workplace comms and because everyone already has WhatsApp installed on their phone. We want to provide our in-house tool to the customer as opposed to having them use, say, Slack, because we can integrate it tightly with our other software tools they are already using, so it would tap into the ecosystem of customer data that enables those features that are tailored to the specific customer and help them get more value out of a tool thanks to that.

What should this tool look like? I'm thinking: Standard interface with sidebar for list of chats. Company-wide chats pinned at the top. Ability to mark favorites that are easily spottable at a glance. Thinking further along, there should be an integration with the shifts calendar or company's official holiday calendar, etc.

Can you Research whatsapp features within a single convo? Also within a group chat?

For the MVP we'll need to scope out the supported features like image upload, file sharing, voice messages with transcript. And we'll need to decide, at a high leve, where to upload images, files, voice recordings and how to transcribe them.

## Brainstorming the product design

Let's run a brainstorming session with you making suggestions or asking for clarifying questions and myself steering the decision making. We'll do 10 rounds as a warm up. The goal is to map out the scope and the feature set of the MVP we're about to build. Following that, I will introduce the tech stack and we'll keep bashing ideas to get closer to an implementation plan.

Your 2nd job is to keep a log of these sessions and by the end of each one produce a new document that will summarizes (1) the  Premise, (2) the key decisions or forks in the road where we backtrack or rethink any of the notions we started with, and (3) the final product vision so far along with the conceptual architecture for the implementation.
